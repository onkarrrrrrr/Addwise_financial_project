Disclosure & Regulatory Information

Important regulatory and compliance information for investors

As a financial services provider, we are committed to transparency and regulatory compliance.

1. Regulatory Status

Addwise Financials operates under applicable securities laws and regulations in India. We maintain the highest standards of compliance and transparency in all our business operations.

2. SEBI Registration

3. Key Information Memorandum (KIM)

Every mutual fund scheme is required to have a KIM that provides essential information about the scheme, including its objectives, risk profile, and performance. KIMs are available on the respective mutual fund websites and with registered distributors.

4. Statement of Additional Information (SAI)

SAI contains detailed information about the scheme's operations, investment strategies, and fund management. You can request SAI from any AMC (Asset Management Company) or download it from their website.

5. AMFI Code of Conduct

We adhere to the Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI) Code of Conduct, which sets ethical standards for mutual fund distributors. This ensures transparency, fair dealing, and investor protection.

AMFI Code of Conduct →

6. Risk Disclosure

Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. The value of your investment may go up or down depending on the performance of the market. Past performance is not indicative of future results.

  • Equity funds carry higher market risk
  • Debt funds have interest rate and credit risks
  • Hybrid funds combine equity and debt risks
  • Money market funds have lower risk but lower returns

7. Fee and Expense Disclosure

All fees, commissions, and expenses are clearly disclosed in the mutual fund scheme's prospectus. We ensure full transparency regarding any charges associated with the funds we recommend.
