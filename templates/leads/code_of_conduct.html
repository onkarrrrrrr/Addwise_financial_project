Code of Conduct

Our commitment to ethical business practices and investor protection

Addwise Financials® adheres to the highest ethical standards and AMFI Code of Conduct.

1. Transparency and Disclosure

We are committed to full transparency in all our dealings. We disclose all relevant information, including:

  • Commission and fees charged
  • Risks associated with investment products
  • Past performance and fund details
  • Our regulatory status and credentials

2. Suitability of Recommendations

We ensure that all investment recommendations are:

  • Suitable to your financial goals and circumstances
  • Based on thorough analysis of your profile
  • Free from bias or conflicts of interest
  • In your best interest as an investor

3. Investor Protection

Investor protection is our top priority. We:

  • Maintain segregated client accounts
  • Follow Know Your Client (KYC) procedures
  • Comply with all anti-money laundering (AML) regulations
  • Secure all client personal and financial information

4. Conflict of Interest Management

We maintain a comprehensive conflict of interest policy that:

  • Identifies potential conflicts
  • Implements safeguards to prevent conflicts from influencing recommendations
  • Discloses conflicts that cannot be eliminated
  • Ensures recommendations prioritize investor interests

5. Fair Dealing and Honest Conduct

We treat all investors fairly and honestly. We:

  • Provide accurate and complete information
  • Do not engage in misleading or deceptive practices
  • Respond promptly to investor inquiries
  • Handle complaints objectively and fairly

6. Competence and Professionalism

Our team members are:

  • Properly licensed and registered with SEBI
  • Regularly trained on market developments and regulations
  • Committed to professional development
  • Bound by strict confidentiality and data protection

7. Compliance and Regulatory Adherence

We strictly adhere to:

  • SEBI regulations and guidelines
  • AMFI Code of Conduct
  • RBI directives on financial services
  • All applicable securities and financial laws

8. Communication Standards

All our communications are:

  • Clear, accurate, and free from jargon
  • Not misleading or deceptive
  • Provided in writing when requested
  • Archived for regulatory compliance

9. Record Keeping and Documentation

We maintain comprehensive records of:

  • Client communications and interactions
  • Investment recommendations and rationale
  • Transactions and agreements
  • Complaints and resolutions

10. Grievance Redressal

We have a dedicated grievance redressal system:

  • Complaints are handled promptly and fairly
  • Resolution timelines are clearly communicated
  • Escalation procedures are in place for unresolved issues
  • Periodic reviews ensure effectiveness of the process

Commitment: We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of conduct and ethics in all our business operations. Your trust and confidence are paramount to us.

Have Concerns?

If you believe we have violated this code of conduct, please report it to us immediately.
